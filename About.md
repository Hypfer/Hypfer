# About me

Generally, I am a firm believer in "show, don't tell", as work doesn't lie, but stories can (though, lately, LLMs have been challenging a lot of the metrics we've used to judge the former).

I would therefore like to encourage you to just look at my work and form your own proper opinion.
I am fairly confident that you can do that and will arrive at a sensible conclusion.

If you however would like to hear my own account and you're fine with the possibility of me being an unreliable narrator, then here it goes:

## About me (by my own account)

I am Sören, probably best known by now by my online handle Hypfer.
I grew up in northern Germany and stayed there. I liked the medium-sized cities there.
I dislike big ones. I never really liked travelling.

A kid to parents that understood tech and thus could not just buy me a computer but also lock it down, that friction forced me to turn to Linux live CDs to bypass all of that.

Born with a brain wired to (exclusively) understand stuff bottom-up and react with strong emotions to stuff that doesn't make sense (and most top-down approaches. The ones our education system loves).
Therefore, had to live with that constant friction and collision with the structures that seemed to work for most peers.
Eventually, learned to channel that into spite and harvest that spite into drive.


### Growing up

Rephrasing the previous paragraph, one could describe me as "Allergic to bullshit".
However, through pain and growing into adulthood, I had to learn that a lot of what seems to just be bullshit is actually load-bearing for society (and often genuinely clever systems design).

It would've helped me a lot to get that explained by someone, but to do so, one needs to first be aware that it is unreal bullshit and understand its role and function.
Which would need good mentors, but they are few and far between. I hope that my writing and thus impact on the world can slightly improve that.


Having forced myself into unsupervised internet access, I grew up on the internet of that era. Including imageboard culture and the culture war.
Unfortunately to some degree ran with some of it like a lost kid would, due to being very vulnerable to the narrative, messaging, etc.
Only later, through further character development and doing better in life in general, I noticed that most of it was just weak idiots and psyops.

### Reaching out

Eventually, I found "my people" in the Chaos Computer Club bubble.
Which, gradually, snowballed together with what drives me into becoming a FOSS maintainer.

Through said maintainership, I was confronted with a never-ending conveyor belt of mirrors of my own dysfunctions I had previously acted out on the world.
Therefore, I was forced to grow as a person and learn to understand myself and other humans better to make things happen.

From first principles then arrived at the realization that many (but not all) opinions deemed "left-ish" are probably objectively correct - with some being terribly false when colliding with the real world outside of academia, hopes, dreams and should've beens.

Through all the social media dysfunction amplification I was left with a burning hatred of tribalism that only got worse over time.
Now spending most of my waking hours trying to understand the various (sub-)systems our reality is made up of.

### Welcome to my myspace page

I am actually too young for myspace.

I like angry angsty internet-y music.

I wouldn't want to bother said workers with my five layers of multi-clause sentences, so I don't think we would really vibe for hanging out, but I guess it would be possible?

I dislike bureaucrats, frauds, demagogues and people in unearned positions of power.
I do not really want to long-term hang out with them. But I might. Somewhat. Possibly to counter-extract value out of them.

It is kinda hard to find people to hang out with ngl.

I like "realpolitik". I like things happening.
Most of all, I like computers. Also, fuck computers.

### Closing thoughts

People tell me that I'm the most self-reflected guy they know, which, while I cannot vouch for it being true is at least something I'd like to strive to be.

I am still very much human though. Still dysfunctional.
Still have ego and emotions pulling in all sorts of unproductive directions.


Lastly, a lot of time I spend on juggling opposing thoughts in my mind.
Like for example how car culture is kinda cool, but it's also very consumerist so kinda bad actually.
Or how shared downloadable/purchasable identities are both necessary and toxic.
